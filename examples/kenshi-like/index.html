DUSTLANDS

A harsh, lawless desert. No quests, no chosen one. Survive, get strong, build something.

Left-click / drag select   Right-click order: move, attack, mine ore, loot corpses, heal ally, talk to traders, use a bed.
WASD/arrows or middle-drag to pan, wheel zoom, 1-9 pick squad member, Space pause, Q/E game speed, B build, H this help.

Limbs get hurt separately. Bleeding drains blood; low blood or a wrecked head/chest knocks you out. Bandages (buy in towns) stop bleeding and heal. Skills level up by use. Bandits will rob a squad they knock out; hounds will eat it.
